🔒 Your Data 🎲 Bangla Ludu

This Privacy Policy explains what information Bangla Ludu (the "Game") collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can have it deleted. We collect only what the Game needs to work — saving your progress, matching you with other players, and keeping it free with ads.

🟡 2. What Other Players Can See

  • Your player name, avatar, level and game statistics are shown to other players in matches, in player profiles and on league leaderboards.
  • Your friends can see whether you are online or in a game.
  • Your e-mail address is never shown to other players.

🔵 3. Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to show ads, including optional reward videos. Google may collect and use your advertising identifier and general device information to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where you allow them. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the Game shows Google's consent message so you can choose.

🔎 Learn how Google uses this data, and manage ad personalization, at Google's Ads Policy page. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

🔴 4. Google Firebase Authentication

E-mail and Google sign-in are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. Firebase stores your sign-in details (such as your e-mail address) to let you log in securely. See Firebase's privacy information.

🟢 5. In-App Purchases

Coin packs are bought through Google Play using Unity In-App Purchasing. Google Play handles the payment; Unity's purchasing library passes the purchase details between Google Play and the Game. Our server keeps the purchase token and the coin pack bought, to prevent the same purchase being used twice.

🟡 6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game shows reminders (such as "your free spin is ready") that are scheduled on your phone. No personal data is sent to us for these reminders, and you can turn them off in the Game's settings or your phone's settings.

🔵 7. How We Use Information

  • To create your account, save your progress, and let you continue on another device.
  • To run online matches, private rooms, friends, leagues and leaderboards.
  • To process and verify in-app purchases and reward videos, and to prevent cheating and fraud.
  • To show ads and keep the Game free.
  • To fix problems and improve the Game.

We do not sell your personal information.

🔴 8. Data Sharing

We share data only with the services that make the Game work: Google (AdMob ads, Firebase sign-in, Google Play purchases) and Unity (in-app purchasing library), as described above, and with other players only as described in section 2. We may also disclose information if required by law.

🟢 9. Storage, Security & Retention

  • Your account and game data are stored on the game server operated by Automata Studios, and travel between the Game and the server over the internet.
  • We keep your data while your account exists. Backups made to protect against data loss are kept for a limited time and then removed.
  • No method of storage or transfer is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

🟡 10. Deleting Your Account & Data

You can delete your account and all data linked to it at any time, in two ways:

In the Game: open Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. Your account, coins, items, statistics, friends and league data are deleted 7 days later. Until then you can still play, and every time you open the Game you are reminded of the date and can choose "Keep my account" to cancel the deletion.

By e-mail:

  1. Send an e-mail to contact.automatastudios@gmail.com with the subject "Bangla Ludu account deletion".
  2. Include your player name and your player ID (shown in the Game under Settings → Account), or the e-mail address you sign in with.
  3. We delete your account and all linked data within 15 days and confirm by e-mail.

Purchase receipts (the store's order token, without any link to your account) may be kept so the same purchase cannot be used twice, and where the law requires it. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, by e-mail.

🔵 11. Children's Privacy

Bangla Ludu is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country) without parental consent. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
